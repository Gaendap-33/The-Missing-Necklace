🕵️ The Missing Necklace

A mysterious case needs YOU to solve it.

📖 The Case

Last night, a valuable diamond necklace disappeared from the mansion during a dinner party.

Four people were inside the house when the necklace vanished. Everyone claims they are innocent...


Your mission: Examine the suspects, discover the clues, and identify who took the necklace!

👤 Suspects

👩‍🍳

Emma

The chef. She says she was preparing dessert in the kitchen.

🧑‍💼

Daniel

The family assistant. He says he was organizing documents.

👨‍🎨

Leo

The artist. He says he was painting in the upstairs room.

👩‍🌸

Sophia

The guest. She says she was talking to other guests.

🔎 Discover the Clues

A plate of dessert was still unfinished. The kitchen was recently used.
A painting was still wet, suggesting someone had been working there recently.
Important documents were scattered across the desk, but nothing appeared stolen.
A small piece of blue fabric was found near the garden entrance.

🧩 Solve the Case

Who do you think took the necklace?
